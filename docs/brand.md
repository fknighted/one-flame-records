# Brand

One Flame Records is an independent reggae and dancehall label in Montego Bay, Jamaica. It also runs Flames Lounge, a creative space with an outdoor recording studio, a gaming lounge, a kitchen and a bar. One app serves all of it.

The look is called **Sound System** (live since 2026-10-09): a Montego Bay dance poster made for the web. Sound black `#0F0D0B`, poster yellow `#F2C230`, flame red `#C8321F`, paper `#FFF7E6` and a small dose of inner green `#2F6B3A`. Headlines are set in Big Shoulders Display and everything people read in Archivo. The public site is loud, with flat blocks of colour and no gradients or soft shadows. The artist portal, label admin, bar till and gamer portal use the same palette in a calm studio look (`src/app/studio.css`).

**The source of truth is [`design-system/`](../design-system/README.md).** If this page and that folder disagree, follow the folder. Voice and copy rules, including what the label must never claim, are in the brand book, not repeated here.
