## Phase 5 send-back — light-field borders (2026-10-05, evening)

**What the verifier found:** the focus outlines passed, but the light-theme field borders did not. A border has to stand out at least 3:1 from **both** the page around the field and the field's own fill. At `ink/50`, the border is drawn over the field's fill, so on a white or bone field it comes out lighter than it would over cream — too light against the cream page: `/gamer-signup` (white fields) 2.65, `/contact` (bone) 2.99–3.01, `/login` (bone) 2.96. Phase 4 had only compared the border against cream, which is why it reported 3.21.

**Change:** `border-ink/50` → `border-ink/60` on every light-theme field that shares the pattern (12 class strings, 5 files). Same brand colour (ink), only the opacity moved. `ink/55` would have cleared by 0.09 at worst, too thin for screen rounding, so `ink/60` is the lowest step with a real margin.

- `src/components/ContactForm.tsx:48,63,76,94` (bone fill)
