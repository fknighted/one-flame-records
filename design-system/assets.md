# One Flame Records logos and imagery

## Imagery

Real photography the brand uses. There is only one so far.

- `hero-bg.jpg` — a night performance at an outdoor Montego Bay venue: string lights, a hand drum, an audience on benches. Shot on a DJI camera. The home page hero, resized here to 1600 × 1200 (the app's copy in `public/` is 1920 × 1440). Always shown under an `ink` layer at 72% with a soft oxblood glow, so `bone` text reads on it.

Not included: the old Flames Lounge hero (`public/flames-lounge-hero.jpg`) is a stock photo of a different bar, from Unsplash. It was taken off the Lounge page on 2026-10-05 and the file is now unused. Never present it, or any stock or AI-generated image, as Flames Lounge, an artist or a show. There are no real Lounge photos yet.

Artist photos and release covers live in the label's own storage, uploaded through the portal and admin, not in the repository.

Write alt text that says who or what is in the photo and where ("Live performance at an outdoor venue in Montego Bay").

## Logos

The One Flame Records logo: an oxblood flame with a forest-green inner flame, printed with a speckled texture, and "ONE FLAME" in a condensed slab. All files are PNG on a transparent ground, copied unchanged from the app. Their ink is oxblood (#8B2A1F) and forest (#3F5A3A) — the lettering is never bone or white.

- `logo.png` — 1080 × 1080. The formal stacked lockup: flame above "ONE FLAME", "RECORDS" between rules, and "MONTEGO BAY • JAMAICA". Used in the public footer, the studio sidebar on desktop, and the social sharing card.
- `logo-1.png` — 2560 × 1400. Horizontal lockup with "RECORDS" and "MONTEGO BAY • JAMAICA". Used on the login page.
- `logo-2.png` — 2560 × 1400. Horizontal lockup with "RECORDS", no place line. The public header logo (80px tall).
- `logo-3.png` — 2560 × 1400. Flame and "ONE FLAME" only. Not used in the app today.
- `logo-4.png` — 2560 × 1400. One-line lockup: "ONE FLAME" then "RECORDS" tracked out. The studio header and mobile sidebar.
- `icon.png` — 512 × 512. The flame alone. The site favicon (`src/app/icon.png`); use it for avatars and very small spaces.

The wide files have a lot of empty space around the art; crop with padding, never into the art.

Best on `cream` or `bone`. On `ink` the oxblood lettering is only about 2.1:1, so set it larger there or use the flame alone. Never redraw, recolour, stretch, add effects, or separate the inner flame. Minimum size 32px tall; keep clear space of half the flame's width on every side.

The files themselves are in this repository (see each note for its path) and in the claude.ai design system linked from `README.md`.
