# Fola Village identity

Frank approved option 11 with a green rim on 26 October 2026. This is the official village logo. Preserve its italic outlined lettering, black drum, gold river, green rim and red/gold/green community dots. The drum without dots or text is its favicon.

Masters and practical guidance: `brand/fola-village/official/README.md`, `fola-brand-guide.pdf` and `fola-print-masters.pdf`. Public web assets: `public/brand/fola-village/`. The original approval reference is retained untouched in the official folder.

Fola has its own mark within the One Flame website. Continue One Flame's Big Shoulders headings, Archivo body type, paper/ink/yellow surfaces and shared header/footer. The logo palette is ink #0F0D0B, gold #F2C230, red #C8321F, green #2F6B3A and paper #FFF7E6. Use the light mark on dark surfaces; maintain proportions and clear space. See the guide for small-size use.

The page lives at `/fola-village`. It tells the community's supplied founding story, river life, food and music direction. Water/power, school meals, bakery and woodworking remain plans. Do not invent opening times, prices, event dates, visitor arrangements or stock/AI images of the village. Real photographs will come from Frank. Enquiries use the existing One Flame contact page, with a request to mention Fola Village.

The intended name connects with drumming; linguistic evidence and its limits live in `docs/fola-village/name-and-language-research-2026-10-26.md`. Do not turn this into an exclusive dictionary claim.
