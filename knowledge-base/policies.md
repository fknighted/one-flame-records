## Short answer

The current Privacy Policy is effective October 2026; the Terms of Service remain dated June 2026. The privacy text covers information you provide and limited technical information for website operation and diagnostics. The label says it does not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties for marketing. You can ask for your data to be accessed, corrected or deleted at any time by emailing contact at oneflamerecords dot com.

## Details

**What is collected.** Information you provide includes newsletter/contact details, artist application materials and contact messages. The October privacy text also describes limited technical information about website operation and errors.

**Website operation and diagnostics.** No payment information is collected directly and the policy says no tracking cookies or third-party advertising pixels are used. Essential sign-in cookies remain. The policy describes Vercel aggregated analytics/page-speed measurements, Sentry error reports with masked text/form inputs and blocked media in error replay, and YouTube/Spotify embeds under their own policies. Do not interpret no advertising tracking as no cookies or no technical data.

**How data is stored.** On cloud infrastructure provided by Supabase, with transactional email sent through Resend. Data is kept as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or until you request deletion.

**Your rights.** Access, correction and deletion on request by email, answered within a reasonable timeframe.

**Newsletter consent.** Submitting an address requests a secure confirmation email; opening its secure link and pressing Confirm subscription within 24 hours activates or reactivates consent. Opening the page alone does not subscribe you. A quiet form response does not establish subscription or delivery. Confirmed subscribers may unsubscribe through the secure link.

**Intellectual property.** All music, artwork, videos, photographs, text and logos on the site belong to One Flame Records or its artists and rights holders. Nothing on the site grants a licence to reproduce, distribute or create derivative works without express written permission. Scraping and redistributing site content is likewise forbidden.

**Artist submissions.** Submitting material grants the label a non-exclusive right to review it for evaluation only. Any signing, ownership or royalty arrangement is governed by a separate written contract. Submission does not guarantee a response or an offer.

**Governing law.** Jamaica, with disputes falling to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Jamaican courts.

## Common questions

**Is there a refund or cancellation policy?** None is published. Nothing is sold on the website, so the question normally concerns the Lounge and must go to staff there.

**Can I use a One Flame song in my video?** Not without written permission. Ask through the contact form and choose "Sync licensing".

## Escalate

Escalate every data deletion request, legal notice, copyright complaint, licensing request, and any question the published policies do not answer. Give the published email address and stop there.

## Do not say

- Do not state a refund, cancellation, exchange or returns policy. None exists.
- Do not interpret, summarise as advice, or add to the legal terms. Quote or point to the published pages.
- Do not grant, imply or negotiate permission to use any music, artwork or logo.
