## Short answer

Flames Lounge is One Flame Records' creative and social space in Montego Bay, Jamaica. It has an outdoor recording studio, a gaming lounge, freshly made Jamaican food and drinks, and a programme of live events. Walk-ins are welcome and no reservation is needed. Opening hours are not published on the website yet, so please check with the Lounge directly before travelling.

## Details

The site describes the Lounge as "where artists come to record, decompress, eat well, and connect — and where the community comes to be part of something real."

**Outdoor recording studio.** A full professional setup in the open air — microphones, monitors and mixing — described as "all the gear, none of the four walls."

**Gaming lounge.** A place to switch off between sessions. Gaming is walk-in only, paid at the counter. See [[gaming-at-flames-lounge]].

**Food and drink.** Cooked to order. The published menu lists:

- Fish fritters — crispy Jamaican-style, made fresh to order.
- Jerk chicken fritters — spiced, smoky and seasoned.
- Vegetable fritters — garden-fresh, light and crispy.
- Steamed vegetables — seasonal veg, lightly seasoned.
- Signature house-made dipping sauces — ask your server what is on that day.

The site adds that beverages are available and to ask your server for the day's selection. No prices are published on the website.

**Live events.** The Lounge hosts open mic nights, artist showcases, DJ nights, listening sessions, watch parties, and private hire. No dated events are currently listed on the site.

**Social.** The Lounge is on Instagram and TikTok as @flamesmobay. The label itself is separate: @oneflamerecords on Instagram and YouTube.

Photographs of the space are marked "photos coming soon" on the site.

## Common questions

**Do I need to book?** No. The site says "Walk-in welcome — no reservation needed."

**Can I hire the space for a private event or showcase?** Yes, private hire is offered. Use the contact form on the website to start that conversation — see [[contact-and-support]].

**How much does the food cost?** Prices are not published on the website. Ask at the Lounge.

## Escalate

Hand to a human for private-hire bookings, event enquiries, catering questions, or anything needing an address, directions or opening hours.

## Do not say

- Do not give opening hours. The site says "Hours coming soon."
- Do not give a street address, phone number, or directions. Only "Montego Bay, Jamaica" is published.
- Do not quote food or drink prices. They are held in the venue's own till system and are not on the website.
- Do not promise a specific event date, performer or DJ. No events are listed.
