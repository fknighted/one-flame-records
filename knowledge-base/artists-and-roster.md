## Short answer

As of the eleventh of August 2026, nine artists appear on the public roster: Bawlinz, Fola Boss, Kahlic, Layd D, Layshaunex, Mystique Fyre, Phayroh, Twiggy, and Vyperxstudiobeat. Each has a page on the site showing their hometown, genres, and any videos or music the label has made public. The roster page is the definitive list, so check it rather than quoting this one if the count matters.

## Details

Hometowns shown on the roster page: Fola Boss is listed as Kingston. Kahlic, Layshaunex, Phayroh and Twiggy are listed as Montego Bay. The remaining artists have no hometown displayed.

An artist page can show:

- Stage name, hometown and genre tags.
- A biography, when the label has entered one.
- Videos featuring that artist.
- A **Music** section listing audio the artist has chosen to make public — for example Kahlic's page lists a demo titled "Speechless how long" with its running time and a download control.
- Links to the artist's social and streaming profiles, when supplied.

Artist pages are database driven. A page fills out as the label adds content through its admin tools, so an artist with a sparse page is simply awaiting content entry, not inactive.

Behind the scenes an artist record carries a status of pending, active or archived. Only artists the label has published appear publicly.

## Common questions

**How do I find a specific artist?** Go to the Artists page in the main navigation, or use the search icon in the site header and type the artist's name. Search covers artists, releases and news.

**Why does an artist have no music or videos on their page?** The label adds content over time. The catalogue is still filling out — see [[releases-and-videos]].

**Can I contact an artist directly through the site?** No. There is no direct artist messaging. Use the label's contact form and it will reach the label team. See [[contact-and-support]].

## Escalate

Escalate booking requests, interview requests, and anything about a specific artist's contract or availability. The assistant should not speak on behalf of an individual artist.

## Do not say

- Do not name an artist as signed unless they appear on the public roster page.
- Do not give an artist's real name, contact details, phone number or location beyond the hometown shown on the site.
- Do not claim an artist has a release, tour date or chart position unless the site shows it.
