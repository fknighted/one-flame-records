## Short answer

Nothing on the One Flame Records website is paid for online. There is no shop, no checkout and no card payment on the site — browsing, subscribing to the newsletter, submitting music and creating a gaming account are all free. Anything you buy at Flames Lounge — food, drinks or gaming time — is paid for in person at the venue.

## Details

Confirmed by inspecting the site and its code: there is no payment provider connected to the website at all. No basket, no checkout page, no card form, no subscription billing.

What is free:

- Browsing artists, releases, videos, news and the Flames Lounge page.
- Subscribing to the label newsletter.
- Sending a message through the contact form.
- Submitting music for consideration.
- Creating a Flames Lounge gaming account — the site says "Free to join."

What is paid for at the venue, in person:

- Food and drinks at Flames Lounge. Prices are held in the venue's own till system and are **not** published on the website.
- Gaming sessions. The till system sells them in two lengths — thirty minutes at three hundred Jamaican dollars and one hour at six hundred Jamaican dollars — but these are venue rates, not website pricing, so confirm at the counter.

All amounts at the venue are in Jamaican dollars and are whole dollars — no cents.

## Common questions

**Can I buy music, merchandise or tickets on the site?** No. The website is a label showcase, not a store.

**Do you accept card at Flames Lounge?** Accepted payment methods at the venue are not published on the website. Ask at the Lounge.

**Is there a membership fee for gaming?** There are no gaming memberships or accounts. You pay at the Lounge counter for the session you play.

## Escalate

Escalate every question about a specific charge, a bill, a refund, or what a customer was charged at the Lounge. The assistant has no access to any transaction and must not confirm or dispute an amount.

## Do not say

- Do not quote food, drink or menu prices. They are not published anywhere the assistant can verify.
- Do not state which payment methods the Lounge accepts.
- Do not offer a discount, deal, package or loyalty reward. None are published.
- Do not describe the gaming session rates as fixed or guaranteed website prices.
