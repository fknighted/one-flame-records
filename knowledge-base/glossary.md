## Short answer

These are the words visitors and staff use around One Flame Records. Recognise them when someone says them aloud, especially the venue name, the artist names, and the music terms — a voice agent will hear them spoken far more often than it will read them.

## Details

**One Flame Records** — the label. Independent, reggae and dancehall, Montego Bay, Jamaica, founded 2018.

**Flames Lounge** — the label's creative and social space in Montego Bay. Sometimes said as just "the Lounge" or "Flames". Its social handle is @flamesmobay, said as "flames mo-bay".

**MoBay** — the common short name for Montego Bay.

**Roster** — the list of artists signed to the label.

**Fritter** — a Jamaican fried savoury cake. The Lounge serves fish, jerk chicken and vegetable fritters.

**Jerk** — the Jamaican spice and cooking style used on the chicken fritters.

**Sync licensing** — permission to use a recording in film, television, advertising or another visual production. One of the four reasons on the contact form.

**Demo** — an unfinished recording an artist sends for consideration or uploads to their portal.

**Instrumental** — a backing track with no vocal, one of the asset types artists upload.

**Session** — at the Lounge, a block of paid gaming time. In music, a recording session. Check which the user means.

**Game time credit** — the balance, held in minutes, on a Flames Lounge gaming account.

**Artist portal** — the private area signed artists log into. Not open to the public.

**Bashment** — Jamaican dancehall party culture; also one of the video style options in the artist portal.

**Lovers rock, rocksteady, roots reggae, conscious music, ska, Afrodancehall** — genres the label works with.

**Artist names, for recognition:** Bawlinz, Fola Boss, Kahlic, Layd D (said "Lady D"), Layshaunex, Mystique Fyre, Phayroh (said like "Pharaoh"), Twiggy, Vyperxstudiobeat.

## Common questions

**Is Flames Lounge the same as One Flame Records?** They are connected. The site calls the Lounge "part of the One Flame Records family". The label signs and releases music; the Lounge is the physical space.

**What does "pressed in Montego Bay" mean?** It is the label's homepage line. "Pressing" is how vinyl records are made, so it means the music is made in Montego Bay.
