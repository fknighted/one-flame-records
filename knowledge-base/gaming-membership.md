## Short answer

Flames Lounge offers a free gaming account. Sign up on the website at the "Join as a Gamer" page with your name and email address, and you will be sent an email invitation to set a password. The account lets you track your sessions, build up game time credit, and skip the queue. Sessions themselves are paid for at the Lounge, not online.

## Details

**How to join.** From the Flames Lounge page, choose "Join as a Gamer". The form asks only for your name and your email address. The site's own words: "Track your sessions, build up game time credit, and skip the queue. Free to join."

**What happens next.** The system sends an invitation email to the address you gave. Follow the link in that email to set a password. After that you can sign in at the site's login page and reach your gamer dashboard.

**Your dashboard shows** your display name, your game time balance in minutes, any session that is currently running, and your recent balance transactions. A separate "My Sessions" page lists your session history.

**Session rates.** Gaming sessions are sold in two lengths and are rung up by staff at the Lounge: thirty minutes at three hundred Jamaican dollars, and one hour at six hundred Jamaican dollars. These rates come from the venue's till system rather than from the website, so confirm at the counter — the site itself does not publish them.

**Balance.** Your balance is held in minutes. Staff add time when you pay, and time is deducted when a session ends. Balances are never allowed to fall below zero.

## Common questions

**Does joining cost anything?** No. The signup page says the account is free to join.

**I signed up but got no email.** Check your spam folder first. There is also a limit on how many signup invitations can be sent from one internet connection in an hour, which can block repeat attempts. See [[troubleshooting]].

**Can I top up my balance online?** No. There is no online payment. Time is added by staff at the Lounge when you pay in person.

## Escalate

Escalate any dispute about a balance, a charge, or a session that was recorded incorrectly. Only Lounge staff can inspect or adjust a member balance.

## Do not say

- Do not promise a free trial, welcome credit, or promotional game time. None exist.
- Do not say a balance can be topped up, refunded or transferred online.
- Do not state the gaming rates as published website pricing — they are venue rates and can change without the site changing.
