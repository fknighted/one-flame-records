## Short answer

One Flame Records is an independent reggae and dancehall label based in Montego Bay, Jamaica. The label records, releases and represents Jamaican artists, and it also runs Flames Lounge, a creative space in Montego Bay with an outdoor recording studio, gaming, food and live events. On the website you can browse the roster, watch videos, read label news, learn about Flames Lounge, and get in touch about signing, press or licensing.

## Details

The label describes itself in its own words as "an independent reggae and dancehall label rooted in the tradition of Jamaican music — artists, releases, and the culture they carry." Its tagline on the homepage is "Pressed in Montego Bay."

The public website has these areas:

- **Artists** — the roster, with a page for each artist.
- **Releases** — the discography, filterable by type and artist.
- **Videos** — music videos and other clips from the roster.
- **News** — posts from the label.
- **Flames Lounge** — the Montego Bay venue and creative space. See [[flames-lounge]].
- **About** — the label's story and what it stands for.
- **Contact** — a form for enquiries. See [[contact-and-support]].
- **Sign with us** — how artists submit music. See [[signing-and-submissions]].
- **Search** — a search box covering artists, releases and news.

There is no online shop. Nothing is sold through the website. See [[pricing-and-payments]].

Signed artists, label staff, bar staff and gaming members log in to private areas. See [[accounts-and-access]].

## Common questions

**Is One Flame Records a real, operating label?** Yes. It is an independent label in Montego Bay, Jamaica, with a public roster of artists and a working website at oneflamerecords.com.

**Can I buy music on your site?** No. The site is a label showcase. There is no store or checkout.

**Are you related to Flames Lounge?** Yes. Flames Lounge is described on the site as "part of the One Flame Records family."

## Escalate

Hand to a human for anything involving contracts, money, legal terms, or a specific person's account. Direct general enquiries to the contact form on the site.

## Do not say

- Do not state a phone number, street address, or opening hours. None are published.
- Do not say music, merchandise or tickets can be bought on the website.
- Do not describe the label as a major label or as part of a larger music group.
