## Short answer

Gaming at Flames Lounge is walk-in only. Ask at the Lounge counter and staff will start a timed session for you. There are no gaming accounts, memberships, stored balances or online booking, and none have been offered since 9 October 2026.

## Details

**How to play.** Come to the Lounge and ask a staff member. They start your session on a station and ring it up at the counter. You pay in person.

**Session rates.** Gaming sessions are sold in two lengths and are rung up by staff at the Lounge: thirty minutes at three hundred Jamaican dollars, and one hour at six hundred Jamaican dollars. These rates come from the venue's till system rather than from the website, so confirm at the counter — the site itself does not publish them.

**Stations.** Staff can assign an Xbox or a Nintendo Switch. Availability is decided at the Lounge on the day.

## Common questions

**Can I get a gamer account or membership?** No. Gaming accounts and memberships were removed on 9 October 2026. Just ask at the counter.

**Can I top up a balance or book a session online?** No. There is no online booking or payment for gaming. Sessions are paid for at the Lounge.

**I was told I had a gamer account before.** The gamer area of the site has been closed. Ask staff at the Lounge for help.

## Escalate

Escalate any dispute about a gaming charge or a session that was recorded incorrectly. Only Lounge staff can resolve it.

## Do not say

- Do not tell anyone they can sign up, join, or hold a balance for gaming; none of that exists.
- Do not promise a free trial, welcome credit, or promotional game time. None exist.
- Do not state the gaming rates as published website pricing — they are venue rates and can change without the site changing.
