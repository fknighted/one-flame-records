## Short answer

The questions people most often ask One Flame Records are how to get signed, where to find the music, what Flames Lounge is, and how to get in touch. Short answers: send links through the contact form, browse the Artists and Videos pages, the Lounge is the label's creative space in Montego Bay with a studio, gaming, food and events, and the contact form is the way to reach the team.

## Details

Each answer below has a fuller version in its own document — follow the links for detail.

## Common questions

**How do I get signed?** Message the label through the contact page, choose "Artist submission", and include a short bio and links to your best work. They aim to reply within two weeks. See [[signing-and-submissions]].

**Where can I listen to One Flame music?** The Videos page has the label's published videos, and some artist pages carry audio the artist has made public. The discography is not published yet — the Releases page says "Releases coming soon." See [[releases-and-videos]].

**Who is on the roster?** Nine artists as of the eleventh of August 2026: Bawlinz, Fola Boss, Kahlic, Layd D, Layshaunex, Mystique Fyre, Phayroh, Twiggy and Vyperxstudiobeat. See [[artists-and-roster]].

**What is Flames Lounge?** The label's creative space in Montego Bay — an outdoor recording studio, a gaming lounge, Jamaican food made fresh, and live events. Walk-ins welcome, no reservation needed. See [[flames-lounge]].

**What are your opening hours?** Not published. The Flames Lounge page says "Hours coming soon." Ask the Lounge directly.

**Do you have a phone number?** None is published. Use the contact form on the website. See [[contact-and-support]].

**Can I buy anything on the site?** No. There is no shop and no online payment. See [[pricing-and-payments]].

**Can I book the space for an event?** Yes, private hire is offered. Start with the contact form.

**How do I join the gaming lounge?** Use the "Join as a Gamer" page. It is free and needs only your name and email. See [[gaming-membership]].

**Where are you based?** Montego Bay, Jamaica. The label was founded there in 2018.

**Do you use tracking cookies?** No. The Privacy Policy states the site uses no tracking cookies and no third-party advertising pixels. See [[policies]].
