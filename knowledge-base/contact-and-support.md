## Short answer

The main way to reach One Flame Records is the contact form on the website's Contact page. It asks for your name, your email address, a reason for writing, and your message. For privacy, data and newsletter matters the label publishes the email address contact at oneflamerecords dot com. No phone number is published.

## Details

**The contact form.** Fields are name, email address, reason and message. Name, email and message are required. The reason list is:

- General enquiry
- Press and media
- Sync licensing
- Artist submission

The form's own note reads: "For press, sync licensing, or artist submissions — use the form below. No attachments needed to start a conversation." Submissions are emailed straight to the label team, and a reply comes back to the address you supplied.

**Email.** The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service both give contact at oneflamerecords dot com for questions about those policies, for unsubscribing, and for requests to access, correct or delete your personal data.

**Social.** The label is on Instagram and YouTube as @oneflamerecords (confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-05; both are linked in the site footer). Flames Lounge has its own accounts on Instagram and TikTok as @flamesmobay.

**Newsletter.** Every public page has a "Stay in the loop" subscription box that takes an email address and sends new releases, events and label news. To stop receiving it, use the unsubscribe link in any email the label sends, or email the address above. The site also has an unsubscribe page where you enter your email address directly.

**Response time.** The only response time the label publishes is for artist submissions: they aim to get back within two weeks. See [[signing-and-submissions]].

## Common questions

**Do you have a phone number?** None is published on the website. Use the contact form.

**Where are you based?** Montego Bay, Jamaica. No street address is published.

**I subscribed by mistake — how do I get off the list?** Click unsubscribe in any email, or email the label at the address above. The Privacy Policy says such requests are processed promptly.

## Escalate

Escalate anything the contact form cannot carry: an urgent matter, a legal notice, a complaint, or a person insisting on speaking to someone. Point them at the contact form and, for policy and data matters, the published email address.

## Do not say

- Do not give a phone number, WhatsApp number, street address, or opening hours. None are published.
- Do not give a personal email address for any member of staff.
- Do not promise a response time for general enquiries, press or licensing. Only artist submissions have a published aim.
